# AI vs ML vs DL Fundamentals



---

## 📚 Concepts

### 🤖 Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- AI is the big umbrella — the science of making machines smart, just like humans.
- **Examples:**
  - Playing chess like a human
  - Talking to Alexa
  - Driving a car on its own
- **Definition:** Any system that mimics human intelligence.

---

### 📊 Machine Learning (ML)
- ML is a subset of AI — machines learn from data and improve over time.
- **Examples:**
  - YouTube recommending videos
  - Netflix predicting your next binge
  - Gmail filtering spam
- **Definition:** Instead of programming every step, we give machines lots of data and let them figure out patterns on their own.

---

### 🧠 Deep Learning (DL)
- DL is a subset of ML — it uses **neural networks** inspired by the human brain.
- Neural networks allow machines to learn complex patterns from large datasets.

---

## 🔄 Traditional Programming vs Machine Learning

**Traditional Programming**
- Rules + Data → Result  
- Manual logic writing

**Machine Learning**
- Data + Result → Rules (Model)  
- Learns patterns automatically
